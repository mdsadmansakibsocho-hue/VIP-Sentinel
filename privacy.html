VIP Sentinel - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

VIP Sentinel is a Discord moderation and security bot. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the bot.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, VIP Sentinel may process Discord server IDs, channel IDs, user IDs, role IDs, message information required for moderation, and bot configuration information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide, maintain, secure, and improve the bot's moderation and security features.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the bot's features, maintain security, or comply with applicable requirements.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by VIP Sentinel. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

Server administrators can remove VIP Sentinel from their Discord server at any time. If you have questions about data processed by the bot, you may contact the developer or support team.

8. Discord

VIP Sentinel operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the VIP Sentinel developer or official support team.
